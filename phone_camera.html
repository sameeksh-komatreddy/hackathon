Setting up
  • For the side camera, stand the phone about 1–2 m to your side at shoulder height, turned sideways (landscape), so your ear, shoulder and hip are all in view.
  • Keep this page open and the phone plugged in. The screen stays on while the camera is running.
  • Nothing is recorded on the phone. Frames go straight to BackTrack on your computer over your Wi-Fi.
